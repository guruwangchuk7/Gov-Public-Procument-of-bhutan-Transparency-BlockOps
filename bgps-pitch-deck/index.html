Demo: Step 1

Agency Publishing.

NDI Login & Wallet Linking
Tender Creation & Document Hashing
On-Chain Publication (Sepolia)
Speaker Script (1:40–2:20):

Let's look at the product. When a Procuring Agency wants to publish a tender, they log in using their Bhutan NDI identity. They link their Rabby wallet, upload the official tender document, and the system immediately generates a SHA-256 hash. This hash is published to the smart contract, creating an immutable timestamp of the original requirements.

Demo: Step 2

Supplier Bidding.

Secure Proposal Upload
Cryptographic Bid Commitment
Tamper-Evident Record
Speaker Script (2:20–3:00):

Next, Suppliers browse active tenders. When they submit a bid, BGPS doesn't just store it in a database. It creates a cryptographic commitment of the entire proposal. This "Bid Hash" is recorded on-chain. This means that once the deadline passes, no one—not even the Agency—can change a bid or replace it with another.

Demo: Step 3

Admin Control.

Identity Verification Queue
Wallet Authorization
System Governance
Speaker Script (3:00–3:40):

The Admin Dashboard acts as the gatekeeper. Using the Verification Queue, admins review NDI proofs and authorize wallet addresses. They ensure that only legitimate agencies and vetted suppliers can participate in the ecosystem, effectively preventing "shell companies" from skewing the market.

Demo: Step 4

Auditor Oversight.

Hash Mismatch Detection
On-Chain Evidence Review
Independent Audit Reports
Speaker Script (3:40–4:30):

The Auditor is the system's watchman. They have access to a specialized dashboard that automatically scans the database against the blockchain. If a tender document or a bid hash in our database doesn't match the record on Sepolia, the system triggers a "Discrepancy Alert." Auditors can trace the entire history of an event through the immutable on-chain audit trail.
