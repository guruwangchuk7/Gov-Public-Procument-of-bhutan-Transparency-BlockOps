The Challenge

Current invoice workflows create weak proof.

Understanding the current invoice management pain points and why blockchain is the answer.

Current Pain Points

Four issues make every payment harder to trust.

01

Duplicate Journal Numbers

Staff repeatedly use the same journal numbers, causing double entries, data conflicts, and confusion in financial records across departments.

02

Unclear Payment Ownership

No clear record of who sent a payment, who verified it, or whether the invoice should be marked as Paid, Unpaid, or Pending.

03

Weak Audit Control

Manual processes create weak evidence trails, making it difficult to prove who entered or verified a record during audits.

04

Spreadsheet Dependency

Disconnected spreadsheets and paper files lead to inconsistent data, repeated entries, and difficulty filtering by country, status, or payment history.

02
Our Solution

A blockchain-integrated invoice management and payment authentication platform.

A comprehensive platform designed for controlled entry, verification, storage, and audit-grade payment evidence.

System Architecture

Four-layer design for separation of concerns, testability, and scalability.

Security Layer

Presentation

React.js keeps invoice forms, dashboards, and search in one controlled interface before data reaches the backend.

  • Layered separation for easy testing and maintenance
  • Role-based access control across all layers
  • Only paid invoices written to immutable blockchain
  • Full operational data stays in PostgreSQL for flexibility
Payment Verification Workflow

10-step controlled process from invoice entry to blockchain commitment.

Step 1: Employee logs in with authorized account

The process begins with a controlled user identity before any invoice data is entered.

Key Principle

Only verified Paid invoices are written to the blockchain. The immutable ledger contains confirmed, audit-grade payment records while PostgreSQL keeps operational flexibility.
